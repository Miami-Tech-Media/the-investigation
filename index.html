Just an honest accounting of what the evidence supports — and you’re welcome here whether you’re certain, skeptical, or somewhere in between.

How to read this site

Every report carries one to three dots showing how settled its claims are. A report can carry more than one — most real questions are part well-evidenced, part contested.

Well-evidenced
Broad agreement among scholars, regardless of theological position
Genuinely contested
Serious scholars land on different sides, with real arguments either way
Unsettled
Beyond what historical evidence can resolve — the line between history and faith
